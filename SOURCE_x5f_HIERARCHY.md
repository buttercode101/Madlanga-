# SOURCE HIERARCHY

## Tier 1 — Primary Official (highest)
- Gazette 53048 (establishment), 53183 (regulations), Presidency proclamations, National Treasury allocations
- Example: source.gazette.53048 — use for mandate, powers, commissioners definition

## Tier 2 — Commission Record (official)
- madlangacommission.co.za /latest, /commissioners, /hearings, rulings published to record, spokesperson Jeremy Michaels statements, one-year statement 17 Sept 2026
- Example: source.commission.latest.day178 — use for 110 witnesses/174 days/26352 transcript pages/105282 doc pages/750 bundles, rulings

## Tier 3 — Sworn Testimony
- Transcripts, affidavits affirmed, statements under oath at hearings
- Example: transcript.2025-09-17.day001 Mkhwanazi, claim.mkhwanazi.121dockets — status untested until corroborated

## Tier 4 — Documentary Verified
- Bank records, forensic reports, phone extractions admitted as exhibits, tender documents with chain of custody, bundle pages
- Example: exhibit.whatsapp.sibanyoni, doc.bank.carrim — requires document_id + chain_of_custody_verified

## Tier 5 — Media Corroborated
- SABC, Daily Maverick, IOL, News24, Sowetan, TimesLIVE where corroborating Tier 1-4, not standalone fact
- Use only for context, never as sole source for claim

## Tier 6 — Unverified / Alleged
- Social media, unverified claims, allegations not yet tested
- May be stored as claim with status untested, never used for finding

## Rule
- Finding requires Tier 1-4, minimum 2 independent sources
- Tier 5 may support but not satisfy finding gate
- Tier 6 never satisfies gate
- In-camera material Tier 3 but restricted access — note restriction
