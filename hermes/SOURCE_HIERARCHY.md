# Source Hierarchy

**Tier 1 — Primary:** Gazettes, proclamations, regulations, official Commission records and government publications.

**Tier 2 — Controlled public record:** stable commission/archive material that reproduces or indexes the record.

**Tier 3 — Secondary:** reputable journalism and specialist analysis.

**Tier 4 — Discovery only:** search snippets, social posts and unsourced material.

Lower tiers may help locate evidence but do not silently substitute for primary evidence.
